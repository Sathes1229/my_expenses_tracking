# QA: member role, 16 November 2026

Functional test of everything a **member** does, on a test copy of the app (same code as the live
server, version `eee23ff`, with a separate test database). The live site was not used for sign-in
or test entries: its data is real, and test entries would change real budgets and alerts. The live
server's health was checked separately (read-only).

Setup: one test owner, four budgets (Groceries RM 600 at 80%, Petrol RM 300 at 80%, Dining RM 200
at 70%, Utilities RM 400 at 90%), five members who joined through invite links (Aminah, Bala, Chong,
Devi, Ehsan), and 16 entries spread over November and October.

## Bugs

| # | Severity | What happens | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **High** | After any error on the transaction form, **Paid with** jumps back to QR and **Budget** to Uncategorised. Text fields keep their values, so a member who fixes the error and saves stores the wrong payment method and budget without noticing. Affects Add, Edit and Scan receipt. | Add manually → choose Card and Petrol → type `abc` as the total → Save → the error shows and the two lists show QR and Uncategorised. |
| 2 | Medium | After **Clear filters** on Transactions, the list shows everything but the Budget filter still shows the old choice. Pressing Apply filters again quietly filters by it. | Filter by Groceries → Apply → Clear filters → the Budget list still says Groceries. |
| 3 | Medium (owner) | In Settings > AI, after **Save models** the list shows "Not chosen" although the model was saved. Saving again clears it. | Choose DeepSeek Flash → Save models → "Models saved." with "Not chosen" shown. |
| 4 | Medium | **Make guest** acts on one click with no confirmation, and it deletes the person's assistant conversation for good. | Settings → People → Make guest. |
| 5 | Low | No upper limit on a total: RM 99,999,999.00 was accepted. A slip of the finger can wreck a month's figures. | Add manually with total `99999999`. |
| 6 | Low | Budget alerts stay after the entry that caused them is deleted ("Utilities is over budget" while Utilities is at RM 0). | Push a budget over, delete the entry, open Notifications. |
| 7 | Low | A total that does not match the items is saved without a warning (RM 20.00 with items adding up to RM 7.40). | Add two items, change the total, Save. |
| 8 | Low | Transactions offers **Next month** in the current month and accepts any future month; Home stops at the current month. | Transactions → Next month. |

Bugs 1, 2 and 3 share one cause: a drop-down list in this React version does not take a new
starting value after the page updates, so a form reset or a filter change brings back the first one.

**Fixed on 16 November 2026** (bugs 1 to 3), in the shared `Select` component. Re-checked in a
browser: Card and Petrol stay chosen after errors on Add and Edit and the entry saves with them;
Clear filters shows All budgets and Any; Settings > AI keeps the saved model after Save and after
saving again. Bugs 4 to 8 are open.

## What passed

**Joining and signing in**
- Invite form refuses an empty form, a taken username, bad characters, a password under 8 characters and two different passwords, each with a clear message.
- A used invite says so when signed out, and asks a signed-in person to sign out first.
- Wrong password and unknown username give the same message; usernames ignore capital letters; signed-out visitors go to sign-in.

**Profile**
- Blank name and a 41-character name are refused; a new name shows in the header at once.
- Password change refuses a wrong current password and different new passwords; afterwards the old password fails and the new one works.
- Theme switch works and survives a reload.

**What a member can open**
- Menu: Home, Transactions, Budgets, Assistant, Profile and the bell.
- Debts shows 404; Settings and AI settings send to Profile; Setup sends home.
- Budgets shows amounts and meters with no Add, Edit or Archive.

**Transactions**
- Add: refuses empty fields, `abc`, 0, negative, 3 decimal places, dates more than a day ahead and a 101-character merchant. Accepts `1,234.50`.
- Items, note and receipt number are saved and shown again on edit; a bad item amount is refused.
- Edit and Delete of your own entry work; Delete asks first ("Keep it" / "Delete transaction") and the deleted entry then shows "not available".
- Another member's entry opens read-only.
- **Security:** a forged edit and a forged delete of another member's entry were both refused ("You can only change transactions you added.").
- Filters by budget, person, payment method and combinations all give the right count and total; bad values in the address fall back to everything.
- Home figures match the entries: spent and left per budget, the near/over banner, spending by budget, and the 8 latest entries.

**Receipt photos**
- JPEG, PNG and WebP upload; the photo shows next to the form and is linked to the saved entry ("Receipt attached").
- The stored photo has the camera details (EXIF) removed and is served as private, not cached.
- A text file named `.jpg`, a GIF, an empty upload and a 16 MB file are refused with clear messages.
- **Security:** signed out gets 401; an unknown photo gets 404; another member can see the photo; a member cannot attach someone else's photo to their own entry.
- With no receipt-reading model set, the page says so and the member types the details in.

**Alerts and assistant**
- Alerts fire at each budget's warning level and again when over, once per level per month; opening Notifications marks them read.
- Assistant says "isn't set up yet" until the owner picks a model, then answers correctly from the household figures in English and Malay (Groceries RM 115 left, Petrol RM 10 over). The member's meter shows a share, not dollars.
- The conversation survives a reload; Clear asks first; a 20,000-character message is refused; the box stops at 2,000 characters.

**Owner actions that affect members**
- Reset password: old password stops working, the temporary one leads only to "Choose a new password" (pages redirect, uploads refused, the temporary password cannot be reused).
- Remove: the member can no longer sign in; their entries stay under their name.
- Member made guest sees only their own entries, no budget figures and no bell; made member again restores everything.

## Not tested this round

- Reading a receipt with AI: the household's DeepSeek key cannot read images.
- Phone-sized layout: the browser pane was hidden and Chrome was maximised. Checked at phone width in earlier rounds and unchanged since.
- Being signed out on a second device after a password change: needs two devices.

App running 12 hours, firewall on, password login off, no security updates waiting, disk 3% of
193 GB, memory 880 MB of 11 GB, data 1.4 MB, HTTPS certificate valid until 13 Feb 2027. The log
holds 7 rejected requests from internet scanners probing the site; none got through.
