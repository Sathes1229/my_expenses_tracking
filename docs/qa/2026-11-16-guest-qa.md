# QA: guest role, 16 November 2026

Functional test of everything a **guest** does, on the local test copy (http://localhost:3199, same
code as the live server, version `908a2f5`). The test copy already held the member QA data: QA Owner,
members Aminah, Bala, Chong and Ehsan (Devi removed), four budgets and 15 entries.

The owner asked to keep every test record. Five guests joined through invite links: Farid, Gita,
Hana, Imran and Jaya. Their entries, receipt photo and assistant conversation stay in the test copy.
Owner actions that change or remove people (Make member, Make guest, Reset password, Remove) ran on a
throwaway copy of the same data, so the main test copy still has all five guests as they were.

## Bugs

| # | Severity | What happens | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low | Asked what is left in a budget, a guest's assistant says it cannot share that, then suggests checking "the budget screen in the app". A guest's Budgets page shows names only, so the advice leads nowhere. | Sign in as a guest, Assistant, ask "How much is left in Groceries and Utilities this month?" |

The assistant gets no budget figures for a guest, so nothing leaks. The cause is the wording that
tells it who is asking: it says nothing else is shared with the guest, but not that the app shows them
no amounts either. One added sentence there fixes it.

## What passed

**Joining**
- The guest invite page says "You are invited as a guest" and that a guest sees the budget names and their own entries.
- The form refuses an empty form, a username a member already has (in any letter case), bad characters, a password under 8 characters and two different passwords, each with a clear message.
- All five guests joined and landed on Home. A used link asks a signed-in person to sign out first and tells a signed-out visitor it was already used.
- A username typed in capital letters still signs in.

**What a guest can open**
- Menu: Home, Transactions, Budgets, Assistant and Profile. No bell.
- Notifications and Debts show 404. Settings and AI settings send to Profile. Setup sends home.
- Budgets lists the four names with no amounts, no meters and no buttons.
- Home shows only "Your latest transactions" and the two add buttons. No budget section, no household total.
- Transactions has no Person filter. A person in the address is ignored. There is no Next month in the current month.

**No household data reaches a guest**
- The page data of Home, Budgets, Transactions, Add, Scan, Assistant and Profile was searched for member names, member merchants, allocations, spent figures and budget states. None were there.
- A member's entry opened by its address shows "That transaction is not available".
- One guest cannot see another guest's entry.
- A member's receipt photo and another guest's receipt photo both return 404 to a guest.

**Entries**
- Add refuses `abc` and a total above RM 1,000,000.00. Card and Utilities stay chosen after each error.
- Saved with each budget, Uncategorised, each payment method, and in October.
- Gita's entry kept its two items, note and receipt number when opened again.
- Imran edited his own entry (RM 15.00 to RM 12.00, new name), and the list total followed.
- Budget filter: Groceries shows Gita's one entry; Utilities shows the "no transactions match" message.
- **Security:** a forged edit and a forged delete of a member's entry were both refused with "You can only change transactions you added." The member's entry is unchanged.

**Receipt photos**
- Hana uploaded a JPEG on Scan receipt. With no receipt-reading model set, the page says so and she typed the details. The entry shows "Receipt attached".
- Her photo is served to her as private and not cached.
- **Security:** Imran sent Hana's photo with his own entry. His entry saved without it, and the photo stayed with Hana's entry.

**Guests and the household**
- Guest entries count against the household budgets. After the guests' entries the household shows RM 1,474.40 spent of RM 1,500.00: Groceries RM 75.00 left, Dining RM 16.30 left, Petrol RM 10.00 over and Utilities RM 10.00 over.
- Jaya and Farid together took Utilities past its warning level and then over. Members got both alerts in the bell, and no guest saw either.
- Members see guest entries with "Added by", can open them read-only, see the guest's photo, and find all five guests in the Person filter.

**Assistant**
- The hint for guests asks about their own spending.
- Asked about what is left, the assistant gives no budget amounts. It shows Farid's own Utilities spending (see bug 1 for the advice it adds).
- Asked in Malay what he spent this month, it lists Farid's two entries and the right total, RM 55.00.
- Asked what Aminah and the rest of the household spent, it says a guest sees only their own spending.

**Profile**
- A blank name and a 41-character name are refused. A new name shows in the header at once.
- Password change refuses a wrong current password and two different new passwords. Afterwards the old password fails and the new one works.
- Jaya's name and password were then set back, so the test sign-ins still work.

**Owner actions on guests (throwaway copy)**
- The People list shows the five guests with Make member, Reset password and Remove.
- Make member is one press. Gita, made a member, sees the budget figures and the bell.
- Make guest asks first and says the assistant conversation will be deleted. Farid, made a member and then a guest again, lost his conversation.
- Reset password: Hana's old password stops working, and her temporary one leads only to "Choose a new password". She then lands on her guest Home.
- Remove: Imran can no longer sign in, and his three entries stay under his name.

## Not tested this round

- Reading a receipt with AI: the household's DeepSeek key cannot read images.
- Phone-sized layout: Chrome was maximised.
- An invite left unused until it expires (7 days).
- Being signed out on a second device after a password change: needs two devices.
